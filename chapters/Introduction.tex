\section*{Introduction}


The computation of the irreducible characters of the general linear group $GL_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$ is a classical topic in the representation theory of finite groups (see, for instance, \cite{green1955characters}). The main purpose of this thesis is to give an introduction to an approach to this problem, as developed in \cite{jing2024characters}. The core idea presented in \cite{jing2024characters} is to establish an isomorphism between the character space $ \mathcal{R}(q) $ and a tensor product $\bigotimes_{f \in \Phi} \Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}(f)$, where $\Phi $ represents the set of orbits of the Frobenius map on the algebraic closure $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_q$ and $\Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}(f)$ is the ring of symmetric polynomials with variables parametrized by the orbit $f\in\Phi$.

We approach this isomorphism in several steps. First, in Chapter 1, all prerequisites in commutative algebra are covered. Then, Chapter 2, we prove that the character space $ \mathcal{R}(q) $ is isomorphic to the tensor product of Hall algebras $ \bigotimes_{f \in \Phi} H(\mathbb{F}_q[t]_{(f)})$. Next, in Chapter 3, which is based on the article \cite{de2006finite} and lectures \cite{kac2013bombay}, the Boson-Fermion correspondence is established, providing the theoretical foundation for defining the space $ \mathcal{F} = \bigotimes_{\phi \in \Phi^*} \Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}(\phi)$, where $\Phi^*$ is the set of orbits under the action of the Frobenius map $F$ on the direct limit $L = \varinjlim M_n^*$. This construction enables the creation of Schur symmetric functions $s_\lambda(\phi)$ through the action of the vertex operator $S(u; \phi)$ on $\mathcal{F}$.

Finally, in Chapter 4, by realizing the ring of symmetric functions $\Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}$ as the free module generated by the power sum symmetric functions $\mathbb{C}[p_1,p_2,\dots]$, a change of basis in $\Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}$ is introduced that provides a new realization of $\mathcal{F}$ as the tensor product $ \bigotimes_{f \in \Phi} \Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}(f) $. Using the vertex operator machinery developed in \cite{jing2024characters}, an isomorphism $\mathcal{R}(q) \rightarrow \bigotimes_{f \in \Phi} \Lambda_{\mathbb{C}}(f)$ is constructed, referred to as the \textit{characteristic map} \cite{jing2024characters}. 

Let $s_\lambda$ be the Schur polynomial associated to the partition $\lambda$. The irreducible characters of $GL_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$ are obtained as the preimages of the polynomials $\prod_{\phi \in \Phi^*} s_{\lambda(\phi)}(\phi)$ under the characteristic map, for \textit{weighted partition valued functions} $\lambda:\Phi^*\rightarrow\mathcal{P}$. Following \cite{jing2024characters}, a general formula for the character table of $GL_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$ is given.
